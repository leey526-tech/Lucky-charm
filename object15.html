OBJECT 15

The Story

Two summers ago, I went to Yunnan with my family and one of the places we visited was Shangri-La. I remember being surrounded by mountains, seeing yaks everywhere, and visiting a huge Tibetan Buddhist monastery covered with colorful prayer flags. While I was there, I bought a Dzi bead from the temple store. The bead is long and oval-shaped, with a dark brown-black surface and yellowish-white patterns across it. There is a hole through the middle so it can be worn like a necklace. I remember being told that it had been blessed and was supposed to bring protection and good luck. I am not a superstitious person but the temple was massive and with the large buddha statues looming above visitors it just felt so real.

I was supposed to have it with me at all times but it doesn't go with my fashion style, so I don’t actually wear it or bring it around with me. I don’t really care if it can bring me luck and protection, but I would never throw it away. Something about throwing away an object that was blessed to give me good luck feels like I would literally be throwing my luck away with it.

So I keep it somewhere safe. It doesn't really do anything, and most of the time I forget that I even have it. But whenever I find it again, I take it out of the bag just to touch it. Maybe it brings me luck, maybe it doesn’t. Either way, no harm in keeping this.
